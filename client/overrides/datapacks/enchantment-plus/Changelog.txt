# Version 5.4

The data pack has been updated for Minecraft 26.2

- Added:

• Added a new enchantment for boots: Bouncing
-   Causes user to bounce back upon contacting a surface.
-   Also decreases air drag for faster traversal.
-   This does not prevent fall damage.
-   Mutually exclusive with Depth Strider, Frost Walker and Lava Walker.
-   Max level is 1. Can be obtained from loot chests, fishing and villager trades.
• Enchantments now work on sulfur cubes.
• Added Calcite and Sulfur blocks to Earthly Gatherer enchantment.


- Changed:

• Block Reach enchantment reach boost decreased from +1 to +0.5 per level (This was way overtuned).
• Entity Reach enchantment reach boost increased from +0.2 to +0.3 per level (Felt like the nerf was too harsh).


- Fixed:

• Fixed a bug that caused Prosperous enchantment to drop incorrect amount of emeralds.
