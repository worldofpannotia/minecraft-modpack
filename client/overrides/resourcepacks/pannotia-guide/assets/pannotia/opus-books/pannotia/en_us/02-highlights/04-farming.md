Farming is mostly about doing less clicking.

## Harvesting

**Right-click a ripe crop** to harvest and replant it in one go. No hoe is needed, and better hoes harvest a larger area.
Breaking a mature crop with a hoe replants it too; hold sneak to turn that off when you are clearing a field.

## Growing

* **Bone meal does more.** It grows nether wart, cactus, sugar cane and vines. Sneak while using it on a crop to grow it
  straight to full size.
* **Kelp is bone meal.** Use it by hand or from a dispenser.
* **Any flower, any biome.** Bone meal on grass can grow most flowers wherever you are.
* **Grass seeds.** Right-click dirt with wheat seeds to make grass.
* **Rain helps.** Crops grow a little faster while it rains.
* **Ice freezes a crop.** A crop planted on soil with ice beneath it stops growing, which is ideal for decoration.

```item
id: kelp
label: Works exactly like bone meal
```

## Keeping your fields

* Melon and pumpkin soil stays tilled after you harvest the fruit.
* Hoes till a bigger area the better they are: **wood 1x1, stone 3x3, gold and iron 5x5, diamond 7x7, netherite 9x9**.
  Sneak and right-click the centre block.

## Horses

Craft a **Horse Expert monocle** and look at a mount to see its health, speed and jump height. Foals are never worse than
their better parent.

```recipe
pattern: [" X ", "X#X", " X "]
key:
  X: gold_nugget
  "#": glass_pane
result: horseexpert:monocle
```
