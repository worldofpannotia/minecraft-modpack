Enchanting in this pack is built to be played with, not endured.

* **No "Too Expensive!"** Anvil costs are flattened so they do not climb with your level, and the limit is gone.
* **No incompatible enchantments.** Put Sharpness, Smite and Bane of Arthropods on one sword. Put every Protection on one
  helmet. Silk Touch and Fortune can share a pickaxe. You still cannot stack two copies of the same enchantment.
* **Enchantments reach more gear.** Bows and crossbows, tridents, maces and even animal armour accept enchantments they
  could not in vanilla.
* **Grindstones move enchantments.** Put an enchanted item and a plain book in a grindstone to copy its enchantments
  onto the book; the item comes back unenchanted. Put an enchanted book on top and a second book underneath to split
  enchantments between books. Curses can be moved or removed too.
* **Spend your own XP.** Sneak and right-click an empty bottle to bottle your experience. Sneak and right-click a
  Mending item to repair it straight from your levels.

> [!TIP]
> Infinity works on buckets. Combine a water bucket with an Infinity book at an anvil and it never runs out.
