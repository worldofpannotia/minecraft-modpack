# World of Pannotia Datapack

The pack's own datapack. It adds mob heads to almost every mob, 204 decorative "Mini" blocks made in a stonecutter, extra drops from the Ender Dragon, husks and shulkers, a handful of block-drop changes, a wider beacon base list, and some crafting recipes (faster copper oxidation, extra dyes, coral conversion, concrete powder from blasting). It is loaded automatically in every world. There are no commands: everything is passive or crafted.


## How to use

| You want... | Do this |
|---|---|
| A mob head | Kill the mob as a player (Looting raises the chance). See [Extra mob drops and mob heads](#extra-mob-drops-and-mob-heads) |
| Mini blocks | Put a full block in a Stonecutter; take 8 Mini heads |
| Netherite scrap | Mine Ancient Debris (no Silk Touch) |
| Elytra / Dragon Egg / Dragon Head | Kill the Ender Dragon |
| Sand | Kill a husk, parched or camel husk |
| Faster copper ageing | Craft with gunpowder + water bucket (see [Recipes](#recipes)) |

## Mini blocks

In a Stonecutter, **1 full block gives 8 Mini blocks**. Mini blocks are player heads named "Mini \<Block\>" (common rarity) with a custom skin texture of the block. Place them like any player head (on the ground or on walls) as small decorations. Breaking one keeps its data (see [Block drop changes](#block-drop-changes)). Each recipe unlocks in the recipe book once the input block is in your inventory.

## Extra mob drops and mob heads

For about 88 mobs the pack replaces the vanilla loot table with a wrapper: vanilla drops are unchanged, and an extra pool adds a head. Heads are custom-skinned player heads named for the mob and matching its variant (colour, biome type, profession, and so on). All head drops need **killed by a player**. Placed on a note block, a head plays the mob's sound. Textures credit: MoreMobHeads by JoelGodOfwar and minecraft-heads.com.

Chance notation: base chance without Looting / chance at Looting I / extra per level above I. "Always" means 100%.

### Guaranteed (100%) heads

Wandering Trader, Warden, Elder Guardian, Camel, Camel Husk, Sniffer, Zombie Horse, Villager (profession-specific; see note), Wither (Wither Head, plus 50% chance of a Wither Projectile Head or Blue Wither Projectile Head, equal weights), Charged Creeper (Charged Creeper Head), Screaming Goat, Blue Axolotl, The Killer Bunny, and a rabbit named "Toast" (Toast Rabbit Head).

### Tropical fish heads

Killing a tropical fish as a player: 10% (Looting I 15%, +2.5% per level above I). The head matches the fish pattern and colours for 22 known variants: Clownfish, Triggerfish, Tomato Clownfish, Red Snapper, Red Cichlid, Ornate Butterflyfish, Queen Angelfish, Cotton Candy Betta, Threadfin, Goatfish, Yellow Tang, Yellowtail Parrotfish, Dottyback, Parrotfish, Moorish Idol, Butterflyfish, Anemone, Black Tang, Cichlid, Blue Tang, Emperor Red Snapper, Red Lipped Blenny. Any other fish gives a plain Tropical Fish head.


### Sheep: the "jeb_" head

Besides the colour head above, the sheep table also references a "jeb_ Sheep Head" table. That head only drops from a sheep named "jeb_" (name one with a name tag or an anvil); unnamed sheep do not drop it.



## Dragon, husk and shulker drops

| Source | Extra drop | Chance / amount |
|---|---|---|
| Ender Dragon | 1 Elytra, 1 Dragon Egg, 1 Dragon Head | Guaranteed on every kill, including repeat kills; no player-kill requirement, no Looting scaling |
| Husk | Sand | 0-2, +0-1 per Looting level; no player-kill requirement |
| Parched | Sand | 0-2, +0-1 per Looting level |
| Camel Husk | Sand | 0-3, +0-2 per Looting level |
| Shulker | Shulker Shell | Exactly 2, plus 0-2 per Looting level. On top of vanilla's separate 1 shell at 50% (56.25% at Looting I, +6.25% per level above I) |

The Ender Dragon's vanilla loot table is empty (XP and the egg on the exit portal come from game code), so these are added items. On the first kill you get the vanilla Dragon Egg on the portal and the dragon's own egg drop.


## Block drop changes

| Block | Change |
|---|---|
| Ancient Debris | Mining it without Silk Touch drops **Netherite Scrap** directly instead of the debris block (Fortune applies, ore-drops formula; explosion decay applies). Silk Touch still drops Ancient Debris. A diamond or better pickaxe is still needed. |
| Brown and Red Mushroom Blocks | Drop their mushroom more often: roughly 40% chance of 1-2 mushrooms instead of about 22%, and Fortune now increases the amount. Silk Touch still drops the block. |
| Jungle Leaves | Sapling chance doubled: 5% / 6.25% / 8.33% / 10% at Fortune 0 / I / II / III (vanilla 2.5% / 2.78% / 3.13% / 4.17%). Sticks unchanged. |
| Player Head (placed) | Breaking a placed player head keeps all its data components (name, lore, rarity), not just the skin and name. This keeps mob heads and Mini blocks intact. |

### Beacon bases

Beacons can also be powered by Copper Block (all four oxidation stages and the waxed versions), Lapis Lazuli Block, Redstone Block, Coal Block, Quartz Block, Smooth Quartz and Amethyst Block, in addition to the vanilla netherite, emerald, diamond, gold and iron blocks.

## Notes

- **Wandering traders** also sell Mini block heads (for an emerald plus the block) and Hermit heads: see [Wandering Trades (Hermit Edition)](wandering-trades-hermit-edition.md). The Miniblock Merchants mod also overlaps with Mini blocks.
- **Lootr:** the wrapper loot tables only affect mob drops and block drops, not chests, so Lootr is not affected (unverified).
- Helper data: the pack defines item tags `pannotia:mushrooms` and `pannotia:netherite_helmets/chestplates/leggings/boots` for its own use; they have no direct player effect.
- A vanilla copy of loot tables (the `vanilla` namespace, 1404 tables) is internal, unchanged vanilla data and gives no gameplay change.
