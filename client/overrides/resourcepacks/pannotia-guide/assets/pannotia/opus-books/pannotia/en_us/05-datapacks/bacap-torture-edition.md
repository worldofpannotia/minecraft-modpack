# BACAP Torture Edition (Blackbird's Torture Chamber)

An add-on to [BlazeandCave's Advancements Pack](bacap.md) that adds several hundred extreme completionist advancements and trophies. It requires the base BACAP and is aimed at players who have run out of goals: long grinds, collection and statistic challenges, plus a few puzzle-style (riddle) advancements.

## How to use

Ignore it until you have made good progress in BACAP. The new tabs appear in the advancements screen (**L**). Stuff and Technical are the more approachable ones; Stats is long-term.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/function blackbirds_torture_chamber:enable_interactable_trophies` | Make trophies interactive (note: the pack author says this disqualifies you from the Advancement Legend role on the BlazeandCave Discord) |
| `/function blackbirds_torture_chamber:disable_interactable_trophies` | Turn that off again |

## What it changes

| Addition | Details |
|---|---|
| Technical tab | About 237 files, e.g. "Armor of the Ages" sets per armor material |
| Stuff tab | About 136 files |
| Stats tab | About 92 files; statistic milestones such as eating, enchanting and fishing |
| Riddle tab | 10 puzzle advancements |
| Extra advancements in base tabs | About 33 added to BACAP's Adventure, Challenges, End, Monsters, Nether, Statistics and Technical tabs (e.g. Netherite Mad Lad, Jockey Jamboree) |
| Trophies | Granted on completing Torture Edition advancements; decorative by default |
| Fanpack content | About 257 files in the `bacap_fanpacks` namespace |

Tab counts are file counts and include helper advancements. Many advancements are tracked by functions (e.g. one per food eaten) to count things vanilla cannot, such as eating every food or collecting every kind of bee, beehive or sulfur cube. Config functions (`coop_update` and per-team-colour variants) support co-op updates for scoreboard teams.

## Notes

- Does nothing without BACAP.
- Difficulty is deliberately extreme, with very long grinds, rare collections and joke or hidden advancements.
