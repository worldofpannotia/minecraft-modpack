# Vanilla Tweaks Crafting Tweaks

A bundle of [Vanilla Tweaks](https://vanillatweaks.net/picker/datapacks/) crafting tweaks (26.2 selection). They add or change crafting, smelting and stonecutting-style recipes. They are passive: just craft. Recipes are server-side, so you will not see new items, only new recipes.

Selected tweaks: unpackable wool / nether wart / ice, craftable blackstone, bundles (rabbit hide), coral blocks, gravel, more bricks / stairs / bark / trapdoors, powder to glass, blackstone instead of cobblestone, straight to shapeless, universal and sandstone dyeing, coal/charcoal to black dye, copper powered rails, rotten flesh to leather, dropper to dispenser, double slabs, back to blocks.

## How to use

Nothing to enable. Use the recipes below at a crafting table or furnace. "Shapeless" means any arrangement.

## What it changes

### Slabs, stairs and building blocks

| Recipe | Inputs | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Stairs (about 57 types: all planks incl. bamboo and bamboo mosaic, stone, cobblestone, mossy, stone/deepslate/tuff/blackstone variants, all bricks, prismarine, purpur, quartz, smooth quartz, sandstone and red sandstone variants, all cut copper stages incl. waxed) | Vanilla stair shape, 6 blocks | **8** stairs (vanilla 4) |
| Stone brick stairs | Plain, chiseled or cracked stone bricks in the stair shape | 8 stairs |
| Purpur stairs | Purpur block only (vanilla also accepted purpur pillar) | 8 stairs |
| Wooden trapdoors (all 12 wood types incl. bamboo, crimson, warped) | 6 planks (2x3) | **12** trapdoors (vanilla 2) |
| Wood / hyphae and stripped versions | 2x2 logs or stems | **4** (vanilla 3) |
| Bricks | 2x2 bricks | **4** brick blocks (vanilla 1) |
| Slab to 2 slabs (47 non-wood types incl. waxed copper, smooth stone/quartz/sandstone, bamboo mosaic) | 1 block (shapeless) | 2 slabs (the vanilla 3-in-a-row recipe giving 6 still works) |
| Smooth stone | 2x2 smooth stone slabs | 2 smooth stone |
| Back to Blocks: slabs | 2 identical slabs side by side (60 types) | 1 full block |
| Back to Blocks: stairs | 4 identical stairs, shapeless (58 types; no cut sandstone/red sandstone/smooth stone) | 3 full blocks |

Together: 6 blocks make 8 stairs and 4 stairs give back 3 blocks; 1 block makes 2 slabs and 2 slabs give back 1 block.

### Blackstone and redstone parts

| Recipe | Inputs | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Blackstone | 2x2: basalt on one diagonal, coal or charcoal on the other | 4 blackstone |
| Polished blackstone | 2x2: polished basalt on one diagonal, coal or charcoal on the other | 4 polished blackstone |
| Dispenser | 7 blackstone + bow + redstone (`###` / `#B#` / `#R#`) | Dispenser |
| Dropper | 7 blackstone + redstone (`###` / `# #` / `#R#`) | Dropper |
| Lever | Stick above blackstone | Lever |
| Observer | 6 blackstone + 2 redstone + 1 quartz (`###` / `RRQ` / `###`) | Observer |
| Piston | 3 planks on top; blackstone / iron ingot / blackstone; blackstone / redstone / blackstone | Piston |
| Brewing stand | Blaze rod above 3 blackstone | Brewing stand (redundant: vanilla already accepts blackstone here) |
| Dropper to Dispenser | Dropper + bow (shapeless) | Dispenser |
| Dropper to Dispenser (shaped) | Dropper in centre, a column of 3 sticks on one side and 3 string on the other | Dispenser |
| Copper Powered Rails | 6 copper ingots + 1 stick + 1 redstone in the powered-rail layout | 6 powered rails (gold recipe still works) |

### Dyes and colour changes

| Recipe | Inputs | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Black dye | 1 coal, or 1 charcoal (shapeless) | 1 black dye |
| Universal Dyeing: candles, concrete powder, glazed terracotta, stained glass, stained glass panes, terracotta | 8 blocks of any other colour (any mix) around 1 dye in the centre | 8 blocks of the dye's colour |
| Remove colour: candles, stained glass, glass panes, terracotta | 8 coloured blocks around 1 ice | 8 plain candles / glass / glass panes / terracotta |
| Sandstone Dyeing: red sand | 8 sand + 1 red dye (shapeless) | 8 red sand |
| Sandstone Dyeing: red sandstone | 2 sandstone + 1 red dye | 2 red sandstone |
| Sandstone Dyeing: smooth red sandstone | 2 smooth sandstone + 1 red dye | 2 smooth red sandstone |

The dye slot also accepts ink sac (black), lapis lazuli (blue), cocoa beans (brown) and bone meal (white). Concrete powder and glazed terracotta have no uncoloured form. Wool, carpet, beds and banners are not covered.

## Notes

- Interactions: the shulker box shapeless recipe and the extra shell drops from [World of Pannotia](world-of-pannotia.md#dragon-husk-and-shulker-drops) make shulkers easier to obtain.
- The 12-trapdoor and 8-stair yields are intentionally generous compared with vanilla.
