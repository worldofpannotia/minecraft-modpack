A grab bag of changes you might stumble across.

* **Hay bales** stop *all* fall damage.
* **Stop the rain.** Place four to eight cauldrons around a log and light it with flint and steel. The cauldrons fill
  and the rain stops.
* **Hand items to a friend.** Sneak and right-click another player to give them your held stack.
* **Dismount.** Sneak and right-click a boat or minecart to eject its passenger without breaking the vehicle.
* **Vein mining.** Hold the excavate key (default `~`) while breaking a block to mine all the connected blocks of the same type, such as an ore vein or a tree.
* **Fires burn out.** Fire stops spreading and goes out after a few seconds, except over netherrack, magma and soul sand.
* **Bigger stacks.** Boats, signs, beds, banners, eggs, snowballs, ender pearls and more stack to 64.
* **Zoom.** Bind the zoom key under *WI Zoom* in Controls.
