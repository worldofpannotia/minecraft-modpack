# Villager Workstation Highlights

Shows where a villager's job site (workstation) is, which helps when fixing trading halls.

## How to use

Stand within 5 blocks of a villager and run:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/trigger vwh_findStation` | Locate the workstation of the nearest villager (within 5 blocks) |

## What it changes

- The nearest villager glows for 10 seconds.
- A heart-particle cloud appears above its workstation for about 10 seconds.
- Chat shows "The workstation is located at [XYZ]: x y z".
- If the villager has none: "This villager does not have a workstation!"

## Notes

- It picks the nearest villager, not the one you are looking at.
- Related mods: Villager Tweaks and Trade Cycling (see [Villagers & Trading](../04-mods/06-villagers-and-trading.md)).
