* **Cycle trades.** Before you trade with a new villager, press **C** in the trade screen to re-roll their offers. You no
  longer need to break and replace the workstation.
* **Follow the emerald.** Villagers follow you while you hold an emerald block.
* **Curing pays off.** Cure discounts and reputation bring prices down, and hitting villagers still hurts your standing.
* **Where is their workstation?** Stand within 5 blocks of a villager and run `/trigger vwh_findStation`. The villager
  glows and hearts float above their workstation.

> [!TIP]
> Zombie villagers cure in a fixed three minutes, and the remaining time is shown on screen.

## New professions

**Miniblock Merchants** adds dozens of professions that sell tiny decorative blocks. Right-click an *unemployed* villager
with a profession's conversion item, which you will find in chests and drops as you explore, to hire them.
