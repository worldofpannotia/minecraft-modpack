# Loot & Drops

This section covers what chests give you, what mobs and blocks drop, and a few mods that change how drops behave. **Lootr** makes structure loot per-player, **The Athenaeum** adds readable books to loot, and the pack's own datapacks add mob heads, dragon loot and other extras on top of vanilla tables.

## At a glance
| Mod / pack | What it does | Side |
|---|---|---|
| Chorus Fruit Drops Nearby | All fruit from a broken chorus plant drops at its base | Server |
| Just Player Heads | Players drop their own head on death; command to spawn heads | Server |

## Chests and structure loot
### Lootr
Loot containers generated from loot tables (chests, trapped chests, barrels, shulker boxes, minecarts, decorated pots, suspicious blocks, item frames) give each player their own inventory, so nobody misses loot or finds an already-looted chest. Containers look different, and change colour from gold to blue once you have opened them. Explore as usual and open loot chests; each player gets their own copy.
- **New:**

| Block | How to obtain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Loot Chest, Loot Barrel, Loot Shulker | Replace loot-table chests, barrels and shulker boxes automatically | Per-player inventory |
| Copper Loot Chest (plus exposed, weathered, oxidized) | Converted from copper chests with loot tables | |
| Decorated Loot Pot, Minecart with Loot Chest, Item Frame with Loot | Converted from loot-table pots, minecarts and item frames | |
| Lootr gravel and sand (suspicious blocks) | Archaeology blocks with loot tables | Brushable per player |
| Centennial Trophy | Unverified | Lang entry only |

- **How it works:** By default breaking a Lootr container shows a warning and requires sneaking; this is configurable (never, always, or break-to-drop-loot). Optional per-loot-table timers can destroy a container after opening (decay) or regenerate its loot after a delay (refresh), shown by particles. Containers already opened before installing Lootr are not converted, and removing Lootr deletes all Lootr containers. 21 advancements cover opening 1/10/25/50/100 containers, each container type and each copper chest variant.
- **Controls:** `/lootr custom` while standing on a vanilla chest turns it into a shared "starter" chest with a per-player copy of its contents (operator only). `/lootr` has more subcommands; see the Lootr wiki.
- **Pack note:** Required on both client and server. No pack config override.

### The Athenaeum
Adds public-domain written books (about 100 titles: Andersen, Grimm, English and Japanese fairy tales; 102 definitions) to stronghold library and woodland mansion chests. They are ordinary written books with a title, author and full text, generated by a loot function and driven by datapacks. Books can be first, second or third editions, with default chances of 5%, 20% and 50% (configurable). The pack author's own mod.

## Mob and block drops
### Chorus Fruit Drops Nearby
Breaking a chorus plant drops all its fruit at the base position instead of scattering it.

### Just Player Heads
Players drop their own head when they die (enabled by default; drop chance defaults to 1.0 and is configurable down to 0.0001). Heads use stored texture data, so they do not cause lag.
- **Controls:** `/jph head <playerName> [amount]` gives a player head (capitalisation ignored, amount defaults to 1); `/jph cache` clears the head texture cache.
- **How it works:** Skins come from the Mojang API and are cached locally by default, so a new skin does not show until the server restarts or `/jph cache` is run. Mojang limits repeat requests for a profile to about one per minute, so a head may occasionally fail to generate when caching is off.

### Pack datapack drops
The **World of Pannotia** datapack ([world-of-pannotia](../datapacks/world-of-pannotia.md)) wraps about 88 vanilla mob loot tables: it runs the unmodified vanilla table, then adds extras. Mobs not overridden (for example the Wither Skeleton) keep pure vanilla loot.
- **Mob heads (More Mob Heads):** Almost every mob (about 250 head variants) drops a custom-skinned head when killed by a player. Heads match the variant (colour, biome type, profession and so on). Chances are low for common mobs (for example Bat 10%, Cod 10%, Cow 1%, Blaze 0.5%) and higher or guaranteed for rare mobs and bosses (Camel, Camel Husk, blue axolotl always). Each chance rises with Looting. Heads placed on a note block play the mob's sound. See the datapack page for the full per-mob table.
- **Dragon drops:** Every Ender Dragon kill drops an Elytra, a Dragon Egg and a Dragon Head, unconditionally, so the dragon can be farmed repeatedly. The first kill gives two eggs, as the vanilla egg still appears on the exit portal.
- **Husks drop sand:** Husk and Parched drop 0-2 sand (+0-1 per Looting level); Camel Husk drops 0-3 (+0-2 per level).
- **Looting shulker shells:** Shulkers drop 2 extra shells per kill, plus 0-2 per Looting level, on top of vanilla's 50% single shell.
- **Tropical fish heads:** 10% (15% at Looting I, +2.5% per level above I), matching 22 named variants (Clownfish, Blue Tang, Moorish Idol and others); other fish give a plain Tropical Fish head.
- **Block changes:** Ancient debris mined without Silk Touch drops Netherite Scrap directly (Fortune applies; Silk Touch still gives debris). Giant mushroom blocks drop mushrooms more often (about 40% chance of 1-2 instead of about 22%, Fortune now helps). Jungle leaves give double the sapling chance (5% base). Budding amethyst drops itself with Silk Touch (nothing without it, as in vanilla). Placed player heads keep all their data when broken, so custom heads stay intact. Beacons also accept copper, lapis, redstone, coal, quartz, smooth quartz and amethyst blocks as bases.

## Also relevant
- **BlazeAndCave's Advancements** (see [Progression & Advancements](../datapacks/bacap.md), [pack page](../datapacks/bacap.md)): about 1,200 extra advancements, many with item rewards, XP or trophies; configure with `/function blazeandcave:config`.
- **Wandering Trades: Hermit Edition** (see [Villagers & Trading](villagers-and-trading.md), [pack page](../datapacks/wandering-trades-hermit-edition.md)): wandering traders sell 29 Hermitcraft heads for 1 emerald (2-5 per trader) and 190 "Mini" block heads for 1 emerald plus the full block (15-21 per trader, 8 heads per trade).
- **Archaeology Tweaks** (see [World Generation & Exploration](world-generation-and-exploration.md)): new suspicious blocks to brush for loot, and the Gentle Touch enchantment.
- **Get It Together Drops** (see [Performance & Technical](../technical.md)): dropped items merge across a larger configurable radius to reduce lag.
- **Mineable Spawner** and **Mineable Trial Spawner** (see [Tools, Weapons & Armor](tools-weapons-and-armor.md)): spawners, trial spawners and vaults can be mined with a pickaxe (spawner settings via `/trigger mineablespawner.config`; trial spawners via `/trigger mineable_trial_spawner.config`); a spawn egg also drops by default.
- **Resource Backpacks** (see [Storage & Inventory](storage-and-inventory.md)): seven backpack tiers (leather to netherite, plus End); found in structure chests and trial chamber rewards, and zombies and creepers can spawn wearing leather or copper ones. Upgrades use shulker shells, so the Looting Shulker Shells pack helps.
